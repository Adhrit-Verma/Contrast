Honestly

Three things worth knowing before you wait for a paid plan.

On coverage

No plan makes this a compliance claim

Automated testing reaches roughly 30–40% of WCAG issues, and 23 of 86 catalogued criteria have a rule that actually runs. Paying more does not change that arithmetic; it changes how much of your site gets looked at and what happens to the findings afterwards.

true on every plan
On the free tier

It is not a crippled trial

The free scanner runs the same deterministic engine the paid plans do. The limit is five pages and three scans an hour — not a reduced set of checks, and not an expiry date.

no expiry
On timing

There is no launch date, so this page does not invent one

Pro and Team need an account system and a payment processor that do not exist yet. Naming a date we could miss would be the first dishonest thing on this site.

no date · no price
