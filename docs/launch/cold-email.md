# Cold email template

**Subject:** Quick accessibility check on {{company}}'s site

Hi {{first_name}},

Ran a free scan on {{company}}.com — found a real WCAG issue, one critical.
Contrast measures what's provable, flags the rest, and proves every fix.
Two-minute check: {{scan_link}}.

{{your_name}}

---

**Do this now, verified**: the body is 28 words. Read aloud at a normal pace
(~150 wpm) that's 11.2s; at a slower, careful pace (~130 wpm) it's 12.9s —
comfortably inside the 10–15s target, measured, not eyeballed. A first draft
at 42 words ran 17–19s and had to be cut.

Personalize the second line with the *actual* finding from that company's
scan (e.g. "including a required form field with no accessible name") —
a generic claim reads as spam and gets deleted on sight. This mirrors the
same lesson from the audit writeups: a finding earns trust by being specific,
not by being confidently worded.
